# Code of Conduct

This project exists so people can use and improve Chenoot without
friction. This document says what that looks like in practice.

## Expected behavior

- Assume the other person is working in good faith, argue about the work
  rather than about the person, and say what you mean plainly
- Accept that a maintainer may decline a contribution and give the reason
  once
- Respect that people arrive with different levels of experience with
  the relevant tools
- Keep discussion on the technical question in front of you

## What is not accepted

Harassment, insults, or attacks on a person rather than their work.
Sustained disruption of a discussion. Publishing anyone's private
information. Sexualized language or imagery. Anything that would make a
reasonable contributor decide it is not worth the trouble to keep
participating.

## Reporting a problem

Report a problem by opening a private security advisory through the
repository, or by contacting the maintainer directly through the address
on the GitHub profile. Reports are read by the maintainer and are not
shared further without the reporter's agreement.

## Scope and outcome

This applies to issues, pull requests, discussions, commit messages, and
anywhere else the project is represented. A first problem usually gets a
private word. Repeated or serious problems lead to comments being
removed, a discussion being locked, or the person being blocked from the
repository.
